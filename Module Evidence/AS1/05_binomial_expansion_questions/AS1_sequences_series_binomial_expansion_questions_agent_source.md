## Solution to Question 3: C2 January 2011 Question 8

**Question ID:** `AS1SequencesSeriesBinomialExpansionQ003`  
**Source:** Mark Scheme PDF p.2  
**Original reference:** C2 January 2011 Question 8  
**Related question:** See `AS1_sequences_series_binomial_expansion_questions.md`, Question 3  
**Marks available:** 10  
**Match confidence:** High

### Official Mark Scheme Solution

| Step | Official working / result | Mark code | Mark awarded | Brief annotation |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | $(1+ax)^n=1+n(ax)+\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}(ax)^2=1-4x+7x^2$ | M1 W2 | 3 | Writes the first three terms. |
| 2 | $na=-4$ | M1 W1 | 2 | Compares coefficients of $x$. |
| 3 | $\dfrac{n(n-1)a^2}{2}=7$ | MW1 | 1 | Compares coefficients of $x^2$. |
| 4 | $a=-\dfrac{4}{n}$ | MW1 | 1 | Rearranges from $na=-4$. |
| 5 | $\left(\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac{4}{n}\right)^2=7$ | M1 | 1 | Substitutes for $a$. |
| 6 | $8n-8=7n$ | - | 0 | Simplifies equation. |
| 7 | $n=8$ | W1 | 1 | Solves for $n$. |
| 8 | $a=-\dfrac{1}{2}$ | MW1 | 1 | Finds $a$. |

### Final Answer

`n = 8, a = -1/2`

### What the Examiner Wanted

The examiner wanted comparison of coefficients after writing the first three binomial terms. The main trap is forgetting that $(ax)^2=a^2x^2$.
