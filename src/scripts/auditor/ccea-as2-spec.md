# AS2
[AS2-QUNITS-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use fundamental quantities and units in the SI system: length, time and mass
[AS2-QUNITS-LO002] demonstrate understanding of and use derived quantities and units: velocity, acceleration, force and weight
[AS2-KIN-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use the language of kinematics: position, displacement, distance travelled, velocity, speed and acceleration
[AS2-KIN-LO002] demonstrate understanding of, use and interpret graphs in kinematics for motion in a straight line: displacement against time and interpretation of gradient; velocity against time and interpretation of gradient and area under the graph
[AS2-KIN-LO003] demonstrate understanding of and use the formulae for constant acceleration for motion in a straight line
[AS2-KIN-LO004] demonstrate understanding of and use the constant acceleration formulae in two dimensions using vectors
[AS2-FORCES-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use Newton's first law and the concept of a force
[AS2-FORCES-LO002] resolve forces in two dimensions
[AS2-FORCES-LO003] demonstrate understanding of and use addition of forces to find the resultant of a system of forces
[AS2-FORCES-LO004] demonstrate understanding of and use Newton's second law, including forces given as 2D vectors
[AS2-FORCES-LO005] demonstrate understanding of and use the gravitational acceleration, $g$, and its value in SI units to varying degrees of accuracy
[AS2-FORCES-LO006] demonstrate understanding of and use weight and motion in a straight line under gravity
[AS2-FORCES-LO007] demonstrate understanding of and use Newton's third law
[AS2-FORCES-LO008] demonstrate understanding of and use Newton's second and third laws to solve problems involving connected particles
[AS2-FORCES-LO009] solve problems involving equilibrium of forces on a particle
[AS2-FORCES-LO010] demonstrate understanding of and use the $F\leq\mu R$ model of friction
[AS2-FORCES-LO011] demonstrate understanding of and use the coefficient of friction
[AS2-FORCES-LO012] solve problems involving the motion of a body on a rough surface
[AS2-FORCES-LO013] solve problems involving limiting friction and statics
[AS2-SAMP-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use the terms population and sample
[AS2-SAMP-LO002] use samples to make informal inferences about the population
[AS2-SAMP-LO003] demonstrate understanding of and use sampling techniques, including simple random sampling and stratified sampling
[AS2-SAMP-LO004] select or critique sampling techniques in the context of solving a statistical problem, including understanding that different samples can lead to different conclusions about the population
[AS2-DPI-LO001] interpret diagrams for single-variable data, including understanding that area in a histogram represents frequency and connections to probability distributions
[AS2-DPI-LO002] interpret measures of central tendency and variation, including standard deviation and variance
[AS2-DPI-LO003] calculate standard deviation and variance of a population or sample, including from summary statistics
[AS2-DPI-LO004] interpret scatter diagrams and regression lines for bivariate data, including recognition of scatter diagrams that include distinct sections of the population, excluding calculations involving regression lines
[AS2-DPI-LO005] demonstrate understanding of informal interpretation of correlation
[AS2-DPI-LO006] calculate and interpret the product-moment correlation coefficient
[AS2-DPI-LO007] demonstrate understanding that correlation does not imply causation
[AS2-DPI-LO008] recognise and interpret possible outliers in data sets and statistical diagrams
[AS2-DPI-LO009] select or critique data presentation techniques in the context of a statistical problem
[AS2-DPI-LO010] clean data, including dealing with missing data, errors and outliers
[AS2-PROB-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use the addition and multiplication laws
[AS2-PROB-LO002] demonstrate understanding of and use the concepts of mutually exclusive events, exhaustive events and statistical dependence and independence
[AS2-PROB-LO003] calculate combined probabilities of up to three events, using tree diagrams, Venn diagrams and two-way tables
[AS2-DIST-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use the binomial distribution as an example of a discrete probability distribution
[AS2-DIST-LO002] calculate probabilities using the binomial distribution
[AS2-DIST-LO003] link binomial probabilities to the binomial expansion and tree diagrams

# A22
[A22-KIN-LO001] use calculus in kinematics for motion in a straight line: $v=\frac{ds}{dt}$, $a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^2s}{dt^2}$, $s=\int v\,dt$ and $v=\int a\,dt$
[A22-KIN-LO002] use calculus in kinematics in two dimensions: $\mathbf{v}=\frac{d\mathbf{r}}{dt}$, $\mathbf{a}=\frac{d\mathbf{v}}{dt}=\frac{d^2\mathbf{r}}{dt^2}$, $\mathbf{r}=\int\mathbf{v}\,dt$ and $\mathbf{v}=\int\mathbf{a}\,dt$
[A22-KIN-LO003] model motion under gravity in two dimensions using vectors
[A22-KIN-LO004] solve problems involving projectiles
[A22-MOM-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use moments in simple static contexts, including rods, ladders and hinged beams
[A22-IMP-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use impulse and momentum
[A22-IMP-LO002] demonstrate understanding of and use the principle of conservation of linear momentum to solve problems involving direct collisions and explosions
[A22-PROB-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use conditional probability, including tree diagrams, Venn diagrams and two-way tables
[A22-PROB-LO002] demonstrate understanding of and use the conditional probability formula $P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}$
[A22-PROB-LO003] model with probability, including critiquing assumptions made and the likely effect of more realistic assumptions
[A22-NORMAL-LO001] demonstrate understanding of and use the normal distribution as an example of a continuous probability distribution
[A22-NORMAL-LO002] find probabilities using the normal distribution
[A22-NORMAL-LO003] select an appropriate probability distribution for a context, with appropriate reasoning, including recognising when a binomial or normal model may not be appropriate
[A22-HT-LO001] demonstrate understanding and use the language of statistical hypothesis testing: null hypothesis, alternative hypothesis, significance level, test statistic, 1-tail test, 2-tail test, critical value, critical region, acceptance region and p-value
[A22-HT-LO002] demonstrate understanding that a sample is being used to make an inference about the population and appreciate that the significance level is the probability of incorrectly rejecting the null hypothesis
[A22-HT-LO003] conduct a statistical hypothesis test for the proportion in the binomial distribution and interpret the results in context
[A22-HT-LO004] conduct a statistical hypothesis test for the mean of a normal distribution with known, given or assumed variance and interpret the results in context
[A22-HT-LO005] interpret a given correlation coefficient using a given p-value or critical value
